# Frontend-Only Telecaller Portal Setup

## ✅ No Backend Needed!

Your telecaller portal works **100% in the browser** with no server required. All data is stored locally in your browser.

---

## 🚀 Quick Start

### Step 1: Open the Portal
Simply open the HTML file in your browser:
```
frontend/public/telecaller.html
```

Or drag & drop the file into your browser.

### Step 2: Generate Sample Leads (Optional)
1. Click **🤖 Generate Sample Leads** tab
2. Customize filters (Product, City, Type, etc.)
3. Click **Run Prospecting Scraper**
4. Sample leads will populate the table

---

## 📌 Three Ways to Add Leads

### **Option 1: Generate Sample Leads** 🤖
- Click the "Generate Sample Leads" tab
- Adjust city, product, niche filters
- Click "Run Prospecting Scraper"
- Gets populated with 50 random sample businesses

### **Option 2: Import CSV File** 📎
1. Click the "Import CSV File" tab
2. Click "Choose CSV File"
3. Select your CSV with columns like:
   - `Name`, `Phone`, `Type`, `City`, `Area`
   - `Contact`, `Email`, `Website`, `Product`

**CSV Example:**
```
Name,Phone,Type,City,Area,Contact,Email,Website,Product
Sunrise Dental Clinic,9988998899,Dental Clinic,Mumbai,Andheri West,Dr. Rajesh Kumar,contact@sunrisedental.com,true,Mediva360
Modern Fashion Store,9876543210,Boutique,Mumbai,Bandra West,Ms. Priya Sharma,priya@fashion.com,false,CRM Software
```

### **Option 3: Add Leads Manually** ➕
1. Click the "Add Lead Manually" tab
2. Fill in business details:
   - Business Name
   - Niche/Type
   - City & Area
   - Contact Person & Phone
   - Email
   - Product Type
   - Website Status
   - Business Size
3. Click **➕ Add This Lead**
4. Repeat as needed

---

## 💾 Data Storage

All leads are saved in **browser's local storage**:
- ✅ Persists across sessions (even after closing browser)
- ✅ No server needed
- ✅ Private to your browser/computer
- ✅ Export anytime as CSV

---

## 📊 Features

### Search & Filter
- **Quick Search** - Search by name, phone, area, niche
- **Filter by Website** - Has website / Missing website
- **Filter by Google Listing** - Claimed / Unclaimed / Needs Reviews
- **Filter by Size** - Small / Medium / Large
- **Filter by Status** - Pending / Interested / Callbacks / Done

### Outreach Tools
✓ **Call Timer** - Track call duration  
✓ **Call Scripts** - AI-generated opening lines  
✓ **Email Templates** - Pre-written pitches  
✓ **WhatsApp Integration** - Send direct messages  
✓ **Call Outcomes** - Log interested/callback/rejected  
✓ **Notes** - Store follow-up notes  
✓ **Callback Scheduling** - Set reminder times  

### Stats Dashboard
- Total leads count
- Interested leads count
- Scheduled callbacks
- Ready to call count
- Missing websites count

## 📤 Export Data

### Export as CSV
1. Click **📥 Export Leads CSV** button
2. File downloads as: `codecrafters_telecall_[city]_[date].csv`
3. Contains all lead data with call history

## 🎯 Workflow Example

1. **Generate** sample leads or **Import** your CSV
2. **Search** for specific leads using filters
3. **Click** a lead to open the sales desk
4. **Start Timer** for the call
5. **Use Scripts** from the portal for pitching
6. **Log Outcome** (Interested/Callback/Rejected)
7. **Add Notes** for follow-up
8. **Send Email/WhatsApp** directly from portal
9. **Export CSV** at end of day for records

---

## ❌ Limitations (By Design)

- Data only stored in one browser (not synced across devices)
- No real-time cloud backup
- Max ~5MB of data per browser (enough for 10,000+ leads)
